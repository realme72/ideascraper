# Investment thesis

> **Not yet written.** This is the slot it goes in.

Every score in `data/analyses/` is a score *against this document*. It has to be
specific enough that 72 and 48 mean different things — the brief names a thesis
so broad that the score is meaningless ("good companies") as an anti-pattern, and
holding the thesis consistently is part of the Scoping & Judgement mark.

## What goes here

1. **The thesis in a sentence.** What this fund backs, narrowly enough to
   exclude things.
2. **Weighted components** that add to 100, each one scoreable from evidence a
   public-source pipeline can actually gather.
3. **What moves each component up or down**, concretely enough that two people
   reading the same evidence would land in the same band.
4. **What this thesis deliberately passes on** — the strongest signal that it is
   a real thesis and not a wish list.

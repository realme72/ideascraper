## What they do

Spine provides a visual, node-based workspace where multiple AI models run in parallel to tackle multi-step research and deliverables (such as market sizing models, slide decks, and competitive analysis) instead of using a standard linear chatbot.

## Team

Ashwin and Akshay met while studying machine learning at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) over 13 years ago. They went through YC in the S23 batch and have spent three years iterating on Spine AI.

## Market

Knowledge workers, strategy consultants, and investment analysts who currently assemble research and analysis manually across search engines, spreadsheets, and standalone LLM chat interfaces.

## Risks

- End users may prefer simple single-prompt chat deliverables rather than navigating and inspecting an explicit visual canvas.
- The product sits in a crowded horizontal 'agentic tools' space without clear differentiation or an obvious single budget line to replace.
- Long-running, token-intensive multi-agent tasks could generate high inference costs that challenge unit economics without clear enterprise contract sizing.

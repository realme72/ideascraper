## What they do

Mount provides specialized commercial liability insurance and automated security scanning for companies deploying autonomous AI agents, covering risks such as model errors, prompt injection, data leaks, and business interruption.

## Team

Mount was founded in 2026 by Fabian Amherd and John Bachmann. Amherd studied machine learning at ETH Zürich, built computer vision models at robotics spin-off MESH, and previously ran a web development firm. Bachmann comes from a multi-generational insurance family and previously built a technical team in Bangalore scaling to $400k in annual revenue.

## Market

Mid-market and enterprise businesses deploying production AI agents. Today they either carry traditional cyber/E&O policies that exclude or fail to price autonomous software risk, or operate unshielded as legacy carriers lack technical tooling to underwrite AI systems.

## Risks

- Traditional commercial carriers and established cyber insurers may update standard policy language and partner with security vendors to underwrite AI liability before Mount achieves meaningful distribution.
- Underwriting catastrophic or unpredictable AI failures without historical actuarial data creates severe risk of unsustainable loss ratios.
- Blending vulnerability scanning and policy underwriting risks friction between developer buyers who remediate code and finance or risk buyers who purchase insurance.

## Open questions

- Are you operating as a licensed managing general agent (MGA) backed by a reinsurance carrier, or are you acting solely as an insurance broker?
- How are you pricing and underwriting policies in the absence of historical loss data for agentic AI failures?
- Who is the primary buyer inside target companies: the Chief Information Security Officer, General Counsel, or Chief Risk Officer/CFO?

2. Public evidence of active policyholders in production or customer testimonials detailing claims/underwriting workflows.

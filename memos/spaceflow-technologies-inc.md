## What they do

SpaceFlow connects to enterprise ERPs, email systems, and spreadsheets to deploy automated AI agents that handle repetitive supply chain tasks. It automates supplier follow-ups, normalizes vendor price quotes, classifies parts and materials, and reconciles phantom inventory directly within existing internal networks.

## Team

SpaceFlow was founded by Emre Isik (CEO), Hakan Enes Aksu (CTO), and Ali Eren Aytekin. Isik studied Computer Engineering at Cambridge and conducted computational linguistics and motor control research at Cambridge and UCLA; Aytekin has a background in deep learning research at Istanbul Technical University and holds a patent in energy storage. The team is technically strong but lacks documented prior operating experience in enterprise supply chain logistics.

## Market

The product targets supply chain, procurement, and operations planning leaders at industrial and mid-to-large enterprise companies. Historically, these workflows are handled by manual back-office teams or custom integration consultants across legacy ERPs like SAP and Oracle.

## Risks

- Positioning as both a technical infrastructure runtime and a vertical supply chain automation tool creates split messaging and ambiguous buying journeys.
- Industrial supply chain workflows carry high error costs, which may limit customer willingness to graduate agents from shadow mode to autonomous execution.
- Enterprise procurement and ERP integrations typically require long, high-friction sales cycles and security reviews.

## What they do

Sprocket is an open-source desktop and command-line assistant that helps engineers design hardware (generating schematics, bills of materials, and assembly guides) and write software, while autonomously purchasing components or software subscriptions online.

## Risks

- The demo site was offline during the public launch, undermining claims of exceptional reliability.
- Initial public enthusiasm was admittedly driven by friends and family accounts rather than authentic developer adoption.
- Autonomous purchasing agents placing online orders for physical parts and SaaS risk severe financial and security liability if actions fail.

## Open questions

- What verification steps prevent the AI from generating flawed hardware schematics or ordering incorrect physical components?
- What budget limits, human-in-the-loop approvals, and security guardrails govern the automated purchasing mechanism?

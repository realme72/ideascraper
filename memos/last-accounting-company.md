## What they do

A full-stack accounting service where automated AI agents handle routine bookkeeping, payroll reporting, bank reconciliations, and VAT filings, before a certified human accountant reviews and signs off on the final financial statements.

## Team

Last Accounting Company was founded by a four-person team in Helsinki: Riki Karjalainen and Sami Laakkonen, who previously built financial data infrastructure at Token Terminal, alongside Veikko Pere (background in BCG, private equity at Intera Partners, and degrees in Finance and Law) and Pietari Virkkunen (background in tech, PE, and VC).

## Market

Targets Finnish startups, scaleups, and SMBs who currently pay recurring fees to outsourced accounting firms alongside legacy accounting software subscriptions. Agentic LLMs and API integrations now make end-to-end reconciliation and document parsing viable without manual data entry.
